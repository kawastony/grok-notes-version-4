## Phase laws

### Law of Active Phase
On A, (v, I) evolve under locked geometry. Intensity expressed; membership fixed. Index does not change.

### Law of Pause Phase
On P1 and P2, membership is free or stuck. v and I are continuous across the interval. Alignment can reattach membership. Resume continues from the same v with the same I under the new geometry.

### Conservation across pause
v⁺ = v⁻, I⁺ = I⁻, b⁺ ∈ B_adm(b⁻, R, α), and Index is unchanged.

### Relative structure rule
Bridge occupancy and early feed rate are set by relative depth, not absolute curvature alone. Relative depth does not set the index.
