# What is established, what failed, what stays live

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** Synthesis. One positive branch opened once. No further small variants.

## A. Positive controls

A sign-changing mass on a chiral discretization binds a zero when the setting matches the defect.

- Jackiw-Rebbi, one dimension: exact zero. Stronger kink, tighter mode. Pair split falls from 0.098 at separation 1.5 to 0 at separation 6.
- Two-dimensional kink, periodic along the wall: zero, weight 0.84 in the wall strip.
- Two-dimensional vortex: zero, core weight 0.61.
- Three-dimensional vortex line, no Wilson term: zero, core weight 0.62. A line, not a point.
- Four-dimensional Gamma_5 kink: gap 0.34 at L=4, zero at L=5.

Chirality of the numerical Jackiw-Rebbi kernel was not resolved.

## B. Hedgehog counts that failed

- Wilson r=1, open cube L=6 to L=12: delocalized pair, core weight about 0.05, stalled near 0.001.
- Spectral flow on the cube and on the ball: avoided crossing, net flow 0.
- Boundary-mass pin: inert.
- r=0 beside the frozen point: gap stays 0.54. No hidden crossing.
- Overlap index, anticommuting grading: 0 at L=4 and L=6, cube and ball.
- Domain wall, L=4, six slices: twelve modes below 0.05, center weight 0.014.
- Radial continuum channel without angular momentum: gap 0.85.

## C. The one positive branch

Continuum radial channel with the angular 1/r piece restored, mass v tanh(r/1.5), 300 points out to 16.

Without 1/r, lowest abs 0.616. With 1/r, lowest abs 1.582, weight inside r<2 is 0. This discretization does not bind. It is one attempt, not a scan. A different angular reduction is not authorized by this note.

## Conjecture, tested class only

On the finite open lattices and the operator families in section B, hedgehog degree on a shell does not force a nonzero spectral index. Geometric degree can be present. The spectral count can be zero. Near-zero pairs or clutter appear instead of one protected core mode. The missing pieces are a sphere at infinity and a Fredholm limit.

This is not a claim that a continuum Callias operator with invertible mass on a large sphere has index zero. That setting was not built. The angular-channel attempt above is not that setting.

## Closed

No more cube, shell, r, or overlap variants. Quantum gravity stays the lowest assessment until a counted core mode exists on an operator that meets the Callias hypotheses.
