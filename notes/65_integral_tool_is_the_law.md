# Integral tool tried as algebra

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Status:** Three postulates restate the law. Cone does not force them.

Integrating the linear radial equation does not change the power. The map from source to field stays linear, so v^2 still scales as M.

The integral reading that leaves the class is three statements:

D(r) = beta M_enc(r)
Phi = sqrt(D)
g = Phi / r

Outside the disk this is g = sqrt(beta M) / r, so v^4 = beta M. That is the baryonic Tully-Fisher law written as definitions. The root and the division by r are the answer. The only content is D proportional to enclosed mass, and the cone metric does not force it.

MOND writes the same relation as g = sqrt(a0 g_b), with a0 declared. Feedback-driven galaxy formation gets M proportional to v^4 from wind energy balance, without a cone. Neither needs this geometry.

What the cone would have to forbid, and does not yet, is a different interpolation or a derived link from Lambda_* to a cosmological scale. Paper 1 already recorded that tying mu to H0 did not improve the galaxy fit. No new prediction is filed here.
