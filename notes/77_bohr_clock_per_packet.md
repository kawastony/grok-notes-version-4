# Bohr clock is per-packet

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Both moves tried. Neither generates the ruler.

The two-state split has a frequency omega = Delta E. With a length d the combination d Delta E squared is an acceleration in lattice units. Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d). At v = 1, that combination at d = 1 is about 1000 times the value at d = 3. It is not the same for every packet. Nothing in the Hamiltonian forces v and d to one pair.

Adding E_c(g) makes partial F / partial g nonzero. The derivative is the occupation times d E_c / d g. The form of E_c is not in the packet. If its cutoff is a free length, the scale is inserted. If the cutoff is a0, a0 is inserted.
