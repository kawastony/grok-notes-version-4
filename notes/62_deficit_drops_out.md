# Cone deficit drops out

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Status:** The radial equation was re-derived from the cone. The deficit cancels. Nonlinear upgrade not run.

Metric with deficit: ds^2 = dr^2 + (alpha r)^2 dtheta^2, theta in [0, 2pi). Volume weight alpha r.

Radial action: integral alpha r dr [ (1/2)(phi')^2 - V + J phi ]. Alpha is constant. It factors out of the variation. The equation is

(1/r) d/dr (r phi') = V' - J,

identical to the flat radial equation. An s-wave does not see the deficit. Note 61 tested a massive scalar, not a cone.

Massless outer region: r phi' = C, so phi' ~ 1/r. If the force is that gradient, v^2 is constant. The mass term replaces C by the K1 tail and kills the flat piece. That was known before the four runs.

What note 61 rules out is one sourced massive linear scalar, with J and mu inserted. It does not rule out the cone, because the cone was not in the equation.

The galactic claim cannot come from the deficit in this radial sector. A cone effect would need angular dependence, a warp that depends on r, or a coupling that uses the compact length. None of those is in the solved model.

Independent observer stays out of the equation. No variable represents it.

Nonlinear V and alternate force maps are not authorized by this note. They would be new structure on the flat equation.
