# Lowest assessment is quantum gravity

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Status:** The number is not raised.

The session assessments put quantum gravity at 17 percent, below destination at 32 percent. Those figures are assessments, not measurements. This note fixes the reason the lowest one cannot move on the present evidence.

## Why it is lowest

A quantum-gravity claim here would need a gravitational observable forced from the operator spine: a curvature correction, a horizon mode, or a derived scale. The spine does not have that map.

The nearest prerequisite was the Callias count. Notes 09 through 13 say the geometric degree is on the shell and the operator does not count it. Spectral flow is 0 on the cube, on the ball, and with the Wilson term removed. The light pair is delocalized. r=1 stays frozen.

Without a counted defect mode, there is no micro object to promote to a gravitational mode. Raising the assessment would be a relabel.

## What would have to land first

One of these, pre-registered, on the same operator:

1. A spectral flow whose net crossing equals N_def, with the mode bound to the core.
2. Or an explicit statement that the index is continuum-only, and a separate forced gravitational observable that does not use this finite-volume pair.

Neither is in the repo. Until one is, quantum gravity stays the lowest assessment and stays in the not-established bin of the ledger.

Destination stays next-lowest for the same reason: the macro bridge was not inherited from a counted mode.
