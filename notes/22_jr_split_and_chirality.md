# Jackiw-Rebbi: split versus separation, chirality unresolved

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** One-dimensional only. Closed higher-dimensional branches stay closed.

## Agreed reading

The detector finds a genuine bound zero when the mass changes sign in one dimension. Stronger kink, tighter mode. That piece is real. It does not reshape the galaxy or cosmology layer. It does not reopen the r=1 Wilson branch, the overlap index of 0, or the domain-wall pile.

## Chirality

Not confirmed. A single-eigenvector expectation of sigma_x, sigma_y, and sigma_z is 0 on both the kink and the antikink. The kernel is twofold. The relative phase between components is not a clean plus or minus pi/2 (kink modes at -2.53 and 1.70 radians). The numerical zero is a mixture. Opposite chirality of the antikink is the continuum statement, not a result of this run.
