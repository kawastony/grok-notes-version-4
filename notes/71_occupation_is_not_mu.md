# Packet occupation is not the interpolation

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Identity checked. Map not forced.

The simple interpolation is logistic in the logarithm of the field:

mu(x) = x/(1+x) = 1/(1+exp(-ln x)), x = g/a0.

The packet split is exponential in wall separation, Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d), with d in lattice units and v the asymptotic mass. The weight on one wall in the two-level subspace is 1/(1+exp(2 v d)) only if that form is written in. The earned exponent is 2 v d. It is not ln(a0/g).

If 2 v d is set equal to ln(a0/g) by hand, the simple function falls out. That is the answer inserted. The packet action does not contain g, and it does not force a logarithm of a field. A linear substitution would give a falling occupation, which is not MOND either. Neither substitution is derived.

The clock comparison is a number, not a bridge. For H0 = 70 km/s/Mpc, c H0 / 2 pi = 1.08e-10 m/s^2. The frozen ruler is 1.41e-10, a factor 1.30. Paper 1 already recorded that tying mu to H0 worsened the galaxy fit. The transfer time is still in lattice units.
