# Cosmological postulate and deep MOND

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Status:** Postulate named. Cone does not force the flux law.

A postulate that the cone relates to cosmology imports the one dimensionful scale the metric cannot make. Pathway 1 already is that import: a_eff = Q^{-1} sqrt(G rho) c, Q = sqrt(2) held, rho = 6.63e-27 kg m^-3, factor 1.13 above 0.69 rho_c. Check, not a derivation. Cone angles do not produce the ruler. Paper 1 already recorded that tying mu to H0 worsened the galaxy fit.

Deep MOND is the algebraic shadow of a quadratic-gradient equation, div(|grad Phi| grad Phi) = 4 pi G rho. In 3D the flux is r^2 g^2 = G M, so g = sqrt(G M)/r and v^4 = G M a0 once a0 is supplied. The interpolation function stays free.

The written cone is 2D. Its massless field already falls as 1/r. A root of that falloff rises with radius. Flatness needs a 3D flux before the root. The cone metric does not force the quadratic kinetic term, and it is not shown to be a cross-section of a 3D field.

Sigma_b^{1/7} stays the frozen galactic fit, gamma = 0.1463, white residuals. It is not inherited from this equation. R_cone as an intercept shift is a normalization, not a new force law.

What the cone adds, if the postulate is kept, is a name for the imported scale and a geometric factor Q. It does not yet forbid a MOND interpolation or derive the half-power.
