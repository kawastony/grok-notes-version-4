# 4D Dirac insulator, kink cut

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** Exploration. Not a second-Chern number. Closed hedgehog branch untouched.

## Operator

Four-dimensional lattice Dirac, four components, no Wilson term. Hops counted once. Mass couples to Gamma_5, the product of the four gamma matrices. Three directions periodic. The fourth is open.

A constant mass is the trivial insulator. A tanh kink in the fourth coordinate is the domain wall whose bound modes are the ancestor of a 3D surface of a 4D insulator.

## Result

| Setup | Lowest abs | Below 0.05 |
|-------|------------|------------|
| L=4, constant m=1.5 | 1.532 | 0 |
| L=4, m=0 | 0.309 | 0 |
| L=4, kink | 0.340 | 0 |
| L=5, kink | 0 | 4 |

The L=4 kink only narrows the gap. At L=5 the kink binds a zero. The four-fold print is the symmetric pair, not four invariants.

## What was not computed

The second Chern number of a 4D Bloch bundle. A bulk invariant. A surface theory on a 3D boundary of a 4D topological phase. This cut only says a Gamma_5 kink on a slightly larger grid binds a mode, in the same family as the 1D and 2D kinks.

It does not transfer to the r=1 Wilson hedgehog.
