# Funnel gives a per-device scale

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Analogy checked. Not a derivation.

Continuity on a cone of half-angle theta and film depth delta gives u = K/s, with K = Q / (2 pi delta sin theta). The convective acceleration is K^2 / s^3. A turbulent balance gives u proportional to the square root of g times a hydraulic radius. Both scales are built from Q, theta, delta, and a drag coefficient.

A universal a0 requires that combination to be the same for every galaxy. The notes do not force it. Making the elongation cosmic returns a0 ~ c H0, which Paper 1 already recorded as a worse galaxy fit.

The packet remains field-blind. The funnel does not put g into Delta E.
