# Deficit metric and the 1/r force

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Status:** Derivation only. No warp scan. No angular solve.

## Metric

Constant deficit: ds^2 = dr^2 + (alpha r)^2 dtheta^2, theta in [0, 2pi). Circumference 2 pi alpha r. Flat space is alpha = 1. A defect is alpha < 1.

Volume weight alpha r. For phi = phi(r),

Laplacian = (1/(alpha r)) d/dr (alpha r dphi/dr) = (1/r) d/dr (r dphi/dr).

Alpha cancels. A radial observable does not see a constant deficit.

## Massless 1/r force

No potential, no source outside the disk. Equation: (1/r) d/dr (r phi') = 0. First integral r phi' = C. So phi' = C/r, and phi = C log r + const.

If the extra acceleration is that gradient, g_phi = C/r. Circular balance v^2/r = g_N + g_phi. Outside the baryons g_N falls faster than 1/r, so v^2 approaches C. That is a flat curve. It is the massless radial log, not a cone effect. C is set by the enclosed source, not by alpha.

A mass term replaces the first integral by the K1 tail. The flat piece dies.
