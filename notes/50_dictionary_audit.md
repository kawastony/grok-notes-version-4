# Dictionary audit

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** No dictionary. Phase dependence not used as a rescue.

The earlier golden-bridge repair let a constant vary by regime inside a map that already existed. Here the map does not exist. Letting v or A depend on a phase would fit the lattice more flexibly. It would not name a metre.

| Observable | Carries a unit in the run | Could be the dictionary | Why not yet |
|------------|---------------------------|-------------------------|-------------|
| Zero-mode energy | no, it is 0 | no | a zero has no scale |
| Split Delta E | lattice energy | the energy entry, if a spacing existed | a is not in the operator |
| xi = 1/(2v) | lattice length | the length entry | v is dimensionless here |
| T = pi/Delta E | lattice time | the clock entry | same missing spacing |
| Prefactor A | measured coefficient | no | not a pure function of v, and dimensionless |
| Chirality | 0 | no | unresolved mixture |
| Middle weight | fraction | no | a probability |

The only object that could open a dictionary is a stated spacing, or a stated mass in energy units, coupled to v. Neither is produced by the kink. The cosmic zero and the Paper-24 ruler remain external. No phase-conditioned conversion was fitted.
