# Two-state sum has no field

**Date:** 2026-10-09

The packet has two near-zero states split by Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d). The sum over those states is

Z = 1 + exp(-beta Delta E),
F = -ln Z.

Delta E depends on the asymptotic mass v and the wall separation d. It does not depend on g. So dF/dg = 0. No interpolation comes out.

The simple function mu(x) = x/(1+x) is already the logistic of ln x. Matching an occupation to it by renaming the exponent is an identity. The n-family is mu(x) = x/(1+x^n)^(1/n). Standard is n = 2. The SPARC form is written as nu(y) = 1/(1-exp(-sqrt(y))), y = g_bar/g0, which is the inverse side of the relation. None of these is the derivative of the packet free energy.
