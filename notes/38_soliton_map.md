# Topological solitons and the packet

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** Map. Mediator not derived. Hedgehog branch stays closed.

A topological soliton is a field profile that cannot be unwound without leaving the vacuum manifold. Its charge is the winding. The fermion packet, if any, is a zero mode bound to that profile. Jackiw and Rebbi, 1976: a sign-changing mass binds one zero mode. Charge Q = [sgn m(+inf) - sgn m(-inf)] / 2.

## Scalar kink

phi = v tanh(x/w), w=1, v=1. Energy density integrated on 2001 points: E = 1.3333, which is 4/3. Topological charge 1. That energy is in lattice units. It is not 18.7 eV.

## 18.7 eV

Still external. The kink energy is 4/3 with no mass unit. The fermion zero is 0. Forcing 18.7 eV through hc/E gives 66.3 nm, as in note 37. Neither the scalar energy nor the zero mode produces that number. A soliton mass formula needs v and a coupling from outside the lattice. They are not in the earned chain.
