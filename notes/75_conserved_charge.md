# Conserved charge is not the ruler

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Tried. Not an acceleration.

An observer unchanged by every phase is a conserved quantity. On the kink branch the conserved objects are the norm, the middle weight, and the split Delta E. All three are in lattice units. None is an acceleration.

Writing a0 = c H0 / 2 pi uses the signal speed and the cosmic rate. For H0 = 70 km/s/Mpc that number is 1.08e-10 m/s^2. The frozen ruler is 1.41e-10, a factor 1.30. Paper 1 already recorded that tying mu to H0 worsened the galaxy fit. The 2 pi is the transfer clock, not a phase sum forced by the packet.

A conserved quantity can be a boundary constant. It is not a response. This reading does not put g into Delta E, and it does not derive the ruler from the packet.
