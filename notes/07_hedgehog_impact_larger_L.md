## What the defect does

The hedgehog mass is beta (x) (v f tau.n), f=tanh(rad/Rcore), v=2, r=1.

Three effects, separated:

1. Periodic images cancel the asymptotic sphere. A single hedgehog on a torus is not Callias data. The periodic multiplet stays O(10^-2) and does not enter the gate.
2. Open boundaries produce a near-zero pair, not one mode. The spectrum is symmetric to the printed precision, so the negative-count stays half the dimension and is not an index.
3. The Wilson term at r=1 lifts an exact zero at these volumes. The pair gets lighter as L grows. It does not reach 1e-6, and it is still a pair.
